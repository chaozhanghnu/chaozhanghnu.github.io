In **January 2025**, **Shaojie Hu**, a member of the **UNSAT Lab at Hunan University**, joined **Vadose Zone Journal (VZJ)** as a **Social Media Editor**. In this role, he helps communicate new research from the journal to a broader audience through social-media platforms, including **X** and **LinkedIn**.

<p align="center">
  <img src="/images/news/2025-01-01-vzj-social-media-editor/vzj-social-media-editor.png" alt="Shaojie Hu listed as Social Media Editor of Vadose Zone Journal" style="width: 90%; max-width: 1000px;">
</p>

**Vadose Zone Journal** is an international peer-reviewed journal focusing on the vadose zone—the region extending from the land surface to groundwater. Established in **2002** by **Rien van Genuchten**, the journal provides an interdisciplinary forum for research spanning soil physics, hydrology, geophysics, biogeochemistry, environmental science, and related fields. It is published by **Wiley** on behalf of the **Soil Science Society of America (SSSA)**.

<p align="center">
  <img src="/images/news/2025-01-01-vzj-social-media-editor/vzj-journal-page.png" alt="Vadose Zone Journal webpage" style="width: 90%; max-width: 1000px;">
</p>

Shaojie conducted both his undergraduate and doctoral studies at **Hunan University** under the supervision of **Prof. Chao Zhang**. His research focuses on **unsaturated soils, poromechanics, and phase equilibrium and phase transitions in porous media**.
