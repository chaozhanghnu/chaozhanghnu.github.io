From **11 to 13 October 2024**, members of the **UNSAT Lab at Hunan University** attended the **2nd Workshop on Future of Machine Learning in Geotechnics and the 5th Machine Learning in Geotechnics Dialogue (2FOMLIG & 5MLIGD)** in **Chengdu, China**.

<p align="center">
  <img src="/images/news/2024-10-13-excellent-abstract-award/conference-banner.jpg" alt="2FOMLIG and 5MLIGD conference banner" style="width: 90%; max-width: 1000px;">
</p>

During the conference, research by **Prof. Chao Zhang, Ziheng Geng, and Guodong Wu** received the **Excellent Abstract Award**, with only three teams selected for this distinction. The award-winning work was titled **“Integrating multi-source information for intelligent detection of ground properties during earth pressure balanced shield tunnelling.”**

<p align="center">
  <img src="/images/news/2024-10-13-excellent-abstract-award/excellent-abstract-award.jpg" alt="Excellent Abstract Award certificate presented to Chao Zhang, Ziheng Geng, and Guodong Wu" style="width: 78%; max-width: 820px;">
</p>

During the meeting, **Guodong Wu** also delivered an academic presentation titled **“Vibration-based Classification Charts for Real-time Ground Property Detection during EPB Tunnelling Process,”** co-authored with **Ziheng Geng** and supervised by **Prof. Chao Zhang**.
