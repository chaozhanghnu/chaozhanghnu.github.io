On **5 January 2026**, **Professor Hyoungsoo Kim** from the **Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)** visited the **UNSAT Lab at Hunan University**.

Professor Kim is a professor in the **Department of Mechanical Engineering at KAIST**. His research spans **fluid mechanics, soft matter, interfacial phenomena, multiphase flows, microfluidics, and transport processes in porous media**.

During the visit, Professor Kim toured the laboratory facilities and exchanged ideas with UNSAT Lab members on ongoing experimental and research work.
