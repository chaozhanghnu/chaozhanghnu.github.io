On **11 January 2026**, **Hunan University** and **China Railway Construction Heavy Industry Corporation (CRCHI)** officially released the **Intelligent Agent for Shield Tunneling Operation, Maintenance and Risk Prevention and Control** in Changsha.

The system was jointly developed by Hunan University's **Advanced Technologies and Intelligent Equipment for Underground Space** team and CRCHI. **Prof. Chao Zhang**, director of the UNSAT Lab, was among the key technical members involved in the development. The intelligent agent integrates **state sensing, risk warning, and intelligent decision-making**, supporting the transition of shield tunneling from experience-driven operation toward data-driven intelligent construction.

<p align="center">
  <img src="/images/news/2026-01-11-shield-tunneling-agent/launch-ceremony.jpg" alt="Release ceremony of the intelligent agent for shield tunneling" style="width: 90%; max-width: 1000px;">
</p>

The intelligent agent can assist with **surface-settlement prediction**, **risk warning for shield/TBM operation**, and **intelligent selection of tunneling parameters**. It has been deployed on super-large-diameter shield equipment serving major infrastructure projects.
