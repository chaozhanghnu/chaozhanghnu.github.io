Members of the **UNSAT Lab at Hunan University**, **Shaojie Hu** and **Yuhan Yang**, received **First Prize** and **Second Prize**, respectively, in the **Student Awards of the American Geophysical Union (AGU) Unsaturated Zone Technical Committee**.

The awards recognized their presentations in the session **“Soil Water Phase Transitions: Multiscale Characterization, Hydrological Modeling, and Implications for Critical Zone Science Poster”**, convened by **Chao Zhang** and **Ning Lu**. The presentations were given at **AGU24**, held from **9 to 13 December 2024** in Washington, D.C.
