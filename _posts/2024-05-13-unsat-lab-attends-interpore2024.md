From **13 to 16 May 2024**, members of the **UNSAT Lab at Hunan University** attended **InterPore2024**, the **16th Annual Meeting of the International Society for Porous Media**, in **Qingdao, China**.

During the conference, **Shaojie Hu** gave a presentation entitled **“Adsorption-Induced Effective Stress in Porous Media.”** The presentation was delivered on behalf of **Chao Zhang**, in collaboration with **Ning Lu** and **Lingyun Gou**.

The conference brought together researchers working on porous media science and engineering and featured oral presentations, poster sessions, and academic exchange.
