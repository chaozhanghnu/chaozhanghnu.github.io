On **5 June 2025**, **Professor Markus Flury** from **Washington State University** visited the **UNSAT Lab at Hunan University**.

Professor Flury is a professor of **Soil Physics/Vadose Zone Hydrology** in the Department of Crop and Soil Sciences at Washington State University. His research focuses on **flow and transport in porous media**, particularly water flow, solute and contaminant transport, and colloidal processes in the vadose zone.

During the visit, Professor Flury toured the laboratory facilities and exchanged ideas with UNSAT Lab members on ongoing research.
