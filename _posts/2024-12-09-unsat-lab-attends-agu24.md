From **9 to 13 December 2024**, members of the **UNSAT Lab at Hunan University** attended **AGU24**.

During the meeting, **Yuhan Yang** presented a poster on **“Nucleation-percolation transition in clay desiccation cracking.”** The poster highlighted how increasing pore-structure heterogeneity can shift the clay desiccation cracking mode from nucleation to percolation.
