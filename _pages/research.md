<h4>Research Theme</h4>
<p><font size=4>"SMARIN" stands for the Sustainable Materials and Resilient Infrastructure. </font></p>
<hr />

<font size=4>Our current research at University of Miami encompasses the following interconnected areas:</font>
<ul > 
<li> <font size=4>Construction of resilient communities and rehabilitation of built environments: e.g., multifunctional coatings and admixtures, recycling and upcycling of byproducts and wastes, and novel use of biochar and other carbon materials; </font></li>
<li> <font size=4>Nanotechnology, molecular science, and electrochemical engineering for urban living, energy innovations and circular economy;</font></li>
<li> <font size=4>Multi-scale engineering and characterization, corrosion protection, preservation & rehabilitation, self-healing concrete, coatings, and functional layers for smart cities</font></li>
</ul>
